Got an email saying your account will be locked in twenty-four hours? Stop. Here are three red flags of a phishing email. One: urgency. Attackers rush you, so you don't stop to think. Two: a mismatched sender. The name says your bank, but the address says something else. Three: a link that doesn't match. Hover before you click, and check the real domain. When in doubt, go to the website directly. Never through the email. Follow Securidge for one security habit at a time.
