# Sources - fake QR code stickers on parking machines ("quishing")

Primary
- Auckland Transport media release, "Aucklanders warned about scam parking meter QR codes" (Sep 8, 2026): https://at.govt.nz/about-us/news-events/media-centre/2026-media-releases/aucklanders-warned-about-scam-parking-meter-qr-codes - scam stickers on a handful of city-centre meters; similar stickers in Wellington and Christchurch; quote "AT does not use QR codes on any of our parking meters or machines."; pay via AT Park app or card at machine; if card entered, contact bank immediately.
- PSNI (Police Service of Northern Ireland), "Police are encouraging drivers to be aware of QR Code car park scams" (Jun 27, 2025): https://www.psni.police.uk/latest-news/police-are-encouraging-drivers-be-aware-qr-code-car-park-scams - fake stickers over legitimate codes lead to sites stealing personal/financial details; advice: check for tampering, use official apps/websites, contact bank immediately if scammed.

Secondary
- Chronicle.lu (Apr 28, 2026): https://chronicle.lu/category/at-home/60906-fraudulent-payment-stickers-found-on-citys-parking-meters - LetzSecure found "SCANNEZ ET PAYEZ" stickers on Luxembourg City meters; fake "ParkPay" page collects card number, expiry and CVV; reported to City, CIRCL and Grand Ducal Police. Corroborated by Luxtoday (Apr 30, 2026).
- Stiftung Warentest / test.de (Jun 25, 2026): https://www.test.de/Gefaelschte-QR-Codes-Betrug-am-Parkscheinautomat-6178561-0/ - City of Hannover and LKA Niedersachsen warnings; stickers over EasyPark codes lead to fake site asking for credit card details.
- Which? (Jun 25, 2025): https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/quishing-scams-warning-how-to-spot-and-avoid-dodgy-qr-codes-asYsH0h6jjP1 - UK council warnings and general advice (background only).
