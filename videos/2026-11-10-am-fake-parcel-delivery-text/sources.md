# Sources - fake parcel delivery texts (parcel smishing)

Primary
- US Federal Trade Commission press release, "New FTC Data Show Top Text Message Scams of 2024; Overall Losses to Text Scams Hit $470 Million" (April 16, 2025): https://www.ftc.gov/node/88093 - consumers reported losing $470 million to text scams in 2024; "The most commonly reported type of text scam was fake package delivery."
- Massachusetts Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation, "Outsmart Scammers This Holiday Season" (page dated 11/10/2026): https://www.mass.gov/news/outsmart-scammers-this-holiday-season - "missed delivery" / "address problem" texts; links lead to look-alike sites asking for personal information or a small "redelivery fee"; "Avoid clicking the link"; check tracking on the carrier's or retailer's own site/app; forward to 7726 (SPAM); report at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
- Action Fraud (UK national fraud reporting centre, City of London Police), "Fake DPD messages lead to over £200,000 in losses since June": https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/alert/fake-dpd-messages-lead-to-over-200000-in-losses-since-june - fake missed-delivery text/email, link to fake site asking a small payment to rearrange delivery; within days a spoofed "bank" call/text tells victim to move money to a "secure" account controlled by criminals; forward suspicious texts to 7726.

Notes
- Action Fraud alert page shows no year; used only for the scam pattern (fee, then fake bank call) and 7726 advice, none of its figures are used.
- $470M is US, calendar year 2024 (FTC Consumer Sentinel); on screen labelled "United States, 2024".
- 7726 is the spam-forwarding short code in both the US (Mass.gov/FTC) and UK (Action Fraud).
- Analyst note ("the small fee is the bait... card details") is Securidge analysis.
- Topic distinction: earlier videos covered quishing parking stickers (2026-11-08) and Hi Mum texts (2026-11-09); this is backlog P3 "parcel-delivery smishing".
