Chrome users: that Relaunch button is not optional. Here's why.

This year Google has fixed at least seven Chrome zero-days that attackers were already exploiting.

The latest, in Chrome's V8 engine, could let an attacker run code inside Chrome's sandbox, just by getting you to load a booby-trapped web page.

A researcher reported it on August sixth. On September ninth, the US agency CISA listed it as exploited, and gave federal agencies until September twelfth to patch.

Analyst note: Chrome downloads fixes quietly, but they only take effect after a relaunch. And Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi share this engine, so update those too.

Tonight: open the menu, then Help, then About Google Chrome. Let it update and click Relaunch. Then do the same in your other browsers.
