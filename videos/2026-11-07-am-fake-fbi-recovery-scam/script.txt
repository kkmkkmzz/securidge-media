Already lost money to a scam? The fake FBI may call next.

On July twentieth, the FBI warned that scammers pose as staff of IC3, its Internet Crime Complaint Center, to target people who were already scammed.

They offer to recover your lost money, and then they ask you to pay.

They use fake IC3 websites, spoofed caller ID, cloned voices, and AI deepfake videos of senior FBI leaders.

The FBI says IC3 never asks for payment to recover money, and it does not contact people by phone, email or social media.

Analyst note: if someone wants a fee to recover stolen money, treat it as a second scam.

Tonight: type ic3.gov yourself, skip sponsored search results, and never pay anyone to get your money back.
