# Sources — 2026-11-08 PM · Evergreen: Passkeys explained

Primary
- FIDO Alliance, "Passkey Index 2026" (PDF, October 2026; results compiled Sep 21, 2026): https://fidoalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Passkey-Index-October-2026.pdf
  - 36% of successful sign-ins use a passkey (n=12), up from 26% in 2025 ("directional": participant group changed)
  - 93% passkey sign-in success vs 78% for passwords and traditional MFA (n=12)
  - 2.8x faster, 27 s saved per sign-in on average ("indicative", n=9); web page says "nearly three times faster"
  - All 8 participants that measure phishing report fewer successful attacks
  - 15 participants incl. Amazon, Google, Microsoft, PayPal, TikTok, WhatsApp
