Windows users: in October, Microsoft fixed over fourteen hundred security flaws. In one month.

Depending on who counted, it's between fourteen eleven and fourteen seventeen. A year earlier, October had one hundred eighty.

That single month beat Microsoft's total for the whole of last year.

Why? Microsoft says AI-assisted research now contributes to finding these bugs. More bugs found means more fixes.

And at least one was already being exploited: a Windows Shell bug that skips the warning on downloaded files.

Analyst note: a bigger update doesn't mean Windows got worse. It means more holes are being found and closed. Microsoft's advice is to install updates as soon as possible.

Tonight: open Settings, then Windows Update. Check for updates. Restart when asked.
