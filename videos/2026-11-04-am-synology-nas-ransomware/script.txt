Got a Synology NAS at home or work? Ransomware is hitting the outdated ones.

On October twenty-ninth, Synology warned that attackers are exploiting a bug it patched back in December 2024, on devices that never updated.

Shadowserver tracks over three hundred eighty thousand Synology devices exposed to the internet. Not all are vulnerable, but the outdated ones are targets.

Victims find their files renamed with a locked extension, and a note demanding payment, reportedly thousands of dollars.

Analyst note: RAID is not a backup. If your NAS holds the only copy, ransomware takes it all. Keep one copy offline.

Tonight: update DSM. Turn on automatic updates. And block the admin page from the internet.
