Drivers, that parking QR code may be a fake sticker.

Scammers stick their own QR code on parking meters. It opens a fake payment page that asks for your card number, expiry date and security code.

This year, drivers were warned about fake parking QR codes in Germany, Luxembourg and New Zealand.

In September, Auckland Transport said: AT does not use QR codes on any of our parking meters or machines.

Analyst note: a QR code hides the web address, so check where it really goes before you type anything.

Tonight: look for a sticker on top, use the official parking app or pay at the machine, and if you typed your card into a strange site, call your bank right away.
