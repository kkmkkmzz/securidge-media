Parents: that Hi Mum, new number text may be a scam.

Scammers message you, often on WhatsApp, pretending to be your child on a new number. They chat first, then ask you to pay an urgent bill, saying they can't get into their online banking.

In April, North Wales Police warned of a twist: texts that pretend to come from a parent who lost their bank card, asking you to cover a bill.

In Australia in twenty twenty-two, eighty-two percent of these reports came from people over fifty-five.

Analyst note: a new number proves nothing. A new number plus an urgent money request is the whole scam.

Tonight, tell your family: call the number already saved in your phone, ask something only they would know, and never send money until you're sure who gets it.
