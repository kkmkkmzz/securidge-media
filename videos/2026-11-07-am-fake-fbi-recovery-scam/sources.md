# Sources - fake FBI IC3 "money recovery" scam

Primary
- FBI / IC3 Public Service Announcement I-072026-PSA, "FBI Warns of Scammers Impersonating the IC3" (July 20, 2026; update to I-04182025-PSA): https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2026/PSA260720 - scammers target people who were already scammed, often posing as FBI personnel offering to recover money; AI deepfake videos of senior FBI leaders promoting a fake IC3 site; spoofed IC3 websites; fake social media profiles, then Facebook Messenger / Telegram; caller ID spoofing and AI voice cloning. Advice: IC3 has no social media presence; IC3 does not contact individuals by phone, email, social media, apps, chat or forums; type www.ic3.gov directly; avoid "sponsored" search results; check the address ends in .gov; IC3 never asks for payment to recover funds and never refers people to companies that do; do not send money to people known only online or by phone.

Secondary
- KY3 / InvestigateTV (Aug 19, 2026): https://www.ky3.com/2026/08/19/fbi-warns-scammers-are-impersonating-its-cybercrime-complaint-center/ - same warning; contact via phone, email, social media, fake IC3 websites and AI deepfake videos; impersonators ask for payment; GuidePoint Security's Paul Keener: "They request money in order to recover money."

Notes
- The backlog listed this as "FBI warning, Aug 19, 2026"; the FBI PSA itself is dated July 20, 2026 (Aug 19 is the news coverage). The video uses July 20.
- The PSA gives no complaint counts or loss figures, so the video states no numbers.
- Analyst note ("a fee to recover money is a second scam / treat any recovery fee as a red flag") is Securidge analysis, built on the FBI's statement that IC3 never asks for payment to recover funds.
