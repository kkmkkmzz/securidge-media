[excited] Outdated iPhone or Android? Opening one website was enough to get robbed.

Security firm Sekoia says hackers it links to Russia's APT29 planted attack code on real websites and fake lookalikes.

They also hijacked an analytics script called Plerdy for about a week, so sites using it passed the attack on.

The code checked each phone first. Outdated ones lost browser cookies, and some lost messages, photos and Wi-Fi passwords.

Apple fixed one of these bugs in May 2024. On November fourth, the US agency CISA flagged it as exploited.

[serious] Analyst note: no zero-days here. Every bug was already patched, and Sekoia says an up-to-date system is safe.

[excited] Tonight: on iPhone, open Settings, then General, then Software Update. On Android, update Chrome. And turn on automatic updates.
